When Is the Right Time to Estate Plan?

Home › Resources › When Is the Right Time to Estate Plan?
By Kristi Day · January 29, 2026

Most people wait too long to start estate planning — and their families pay for it later. The honest answer to "when?" is: probably sooner than you think. Estate planning isn't just for the wealthy or the nearly retired.

Waiting lets assets get tangled and options shrink. Gifting and charitable strategies work best with time, and without a plan your family may face probate. A plan puts a trusted person in charge if you can't handle money or medical decisions, and written wishes reduce family conflict.

Update your plan when you experience

  • Marriage or divorce
  • A new child or grandchild
  • Buying a home, rental, or business
  • A move to another state
  • Health changes
  • New tax or estate laws

The core documents

  • Revocable living trust
  • Pour-over will with guardianship nomination
  • Financial and healthcare powers of attorney
  • Advance healthcare directive and HIPAA release
  • Up-to-date beneficiary designations
