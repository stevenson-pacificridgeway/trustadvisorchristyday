Home › States Covered

Moved out of California — or have parents in Arizona, kids in Texas, or a cabin in Oregon? Kristi works with a network of vetted, state-licensed attorneys, so the same complete living trust package is available almost anywhere in the country.

Every plan is drafted to the trust and property laws of your home state, with virtual consultations and signing. The only exception is Louisiana, whose civil-law system works differently.
