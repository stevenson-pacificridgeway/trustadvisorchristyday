California Probate Cost Calculator (2026)

Home › Resources › California Probate Cost Calculator (2026)
By Kristi Day · October 4, 2026

California sets probate attorney and executor fees by law (Probate Code §§10800 and 10810). They're based on the gross value of the estate — debts and mortgages are not subtracted. Enter your numbers below to estimate what your family could pay.

Example: a $1,000,000 gross estate means $23,000 for the attorney and $23,000 for the executor — $46,000 before court costs, appraisal, bond, and any "extraordinary" fees. And probate typically takes 12–18 months.

Estates under $208,850 (since April 1, 2025) can often use a simplified small-estate procedure, and a primary residence up to $750,000 may qualify for a simplified petition. Above that, a funded living trust is the most common way to avoid probate entirely.

This calculator is an estimate for general education, not legal advice. Actual costs vary.
