Why Oceanside families choose a living trust

  • Skip probate court. Without a trust, a California estate over $208,850 generally goes through probate — typically 12–18 months, with statutory fees based on the gross value of the estate. On a $1,000,000 estate, attorney and executor fees alone total about $46,000.
  • Keep it private. Probate filings are public. A trust keeps your family's finances out of the court record.
  • Plan for incapacity. Your successor trustee can manage things if you can't — no conservatorship needed.
  • Protect your children. Name guardians and set when and how they inherit.

What's included

A complete plan includes an attorney-prepared revocable living trust, pour-over will with guardianship nomination, financial and healthcare powers of attorney, advance healthcare directive and HIPAA release, notarization, and help transferring your Oceanside home into the trust — including recording the new deed with the San Diego County Assessor/Recorder/County Clerk.
